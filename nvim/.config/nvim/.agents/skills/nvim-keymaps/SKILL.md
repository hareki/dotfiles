# Keymaps

1. Read [`docs/conventions/keymaps.md`](../../../docs/conventions/keymaps.md).
2. Before finishing, check every `desc` you wrote or changed word by word against the title-case rule. In a review, a capitalized "Under" or "After" is correct, and the lowercase one is the violation.
